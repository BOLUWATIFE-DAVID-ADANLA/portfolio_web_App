## The problem

Most "AI support bot" demos fall apart the moment a business needs to trust the answer. The goal here is a retrieval-augmented assistant that only answers from a real, versioned knowledge base, cites what it used, and fails loudly instead of guessing when it doesn't know.

## Where this stands now

Actively in progress. The write-up will cover the retrieval pipeline design, chunking and indexing decisions, how the agent is grounded against hallucination, and what it took to wire this into a Spring Boot service that a business could actually run in production.
