## The problem

Manual lead triage doesn't scale, and most "automation" fixes just move the busywork around instead of removing it. This workflow qualifies and scores inbound leads the moment they land, cross-references existing CRM data, and routes only the ones worth a human's time.

## Where this stands now

Actively in progress. The write-up will cover the workflow architecture, why n8n over a fully custom backend for this piece, the error-handling and retry decisions that keep it reliable, and how the LLM step is scoped so it only handles judgment calls, not the plumbing.
