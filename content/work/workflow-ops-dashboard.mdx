## The problem

Once automations are load-bearing, someone needs visibility into whether they actually ran. This dashboard surfaces n8n execution history, failures, and retries in one place, built for the person who owns the automation, not just the person who built it.

## The plan

Once built, the write-up will cover the data model for tracking runs, how failure alerting is designed, and the trade-offs of building a thin operational layer on top of a tool like n8n instead of replacing it outright.
